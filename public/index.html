🤔 Needs Your Review

While syncing, these events looked like they might already exist on the destination calendar, so they were held back instead of copied. Compare the two and choose what to do:

  • Same event — they're the same thing. Nothing new is created; the two are linked so future edits follow along.
  • Different event — not a duplicate. A new copy is created on the destination calendar.
  • Skip — don't copy it, and don't ask again.

🔍 Duplicate Scanner

Finds events that appear more than once on the same calendar (for example, leftovers from an older sync tool).
  1. Click Scan for Duplicates. Nothing is changed by scanning.
  2. For each group, the KEEP copy stays. Copies marked DELETE are ticked for removal — untick any you want to keep.
  3. When you're happy, click the orange Delete selected bar at the bottom. Deleted events can't be restored from here.
